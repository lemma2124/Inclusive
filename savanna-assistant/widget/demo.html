Inclusive Assistant demo

Ask a question about the sample course. Try English or Amharic, the voice button, and "Read aloud".

Human help: ask something off-topic such as "who won the world cup", press Talk to a person, then answer as the support person at /agent in another tab.

Interview accommodations: press Request interview accommodations, describe what you need (no medical details required), and answer from /agent. The request shows there tagged "Accommodation".

Install on any page

Two lines. No build step.

<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/widget/inclusive-assistant.v1.js" defer></script>
<inclusive-assistant lang="en"></inclusive-assistant>
