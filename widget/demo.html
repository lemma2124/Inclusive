Languages

Chat replies: English, Amharic, Tigrinya, Afaan Oromo, Somali, and Arabic.

Reviewed course sources are currently in English and Amharic. Other-language replies are generated from those cited sources. Voice transcription is available in English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and Tigrinya.

Add the assistant to a website

Embed the web component with two lines. No build step is required. It floats bottom-right by default; add mode="inline" to dock it in place.

<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/widget/inclusive-assistant.v1.js" defer></script>
    <inclusive-assistant lang="en"></inclusive-assistant>
